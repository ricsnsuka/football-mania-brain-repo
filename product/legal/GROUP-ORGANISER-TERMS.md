These terms add to the [Terms of Service](TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md). They apply when you create a group,
and to anyone who later becomes its administrator. A group holds information about other people, so
running one comes with responsibilities that having an account does not.

## 1. Your group

You are responsible for your group: who joins, who gets which role, and what is recorded in it. The
group's administrators speak for it, and we act on what they do in the app.

Keep at least one administrator. If you want to leave, hand the role to someone else first. Only an
administrator can appoint another.

## 2. Other people's data

For your group's competition data, the group decides what happens to it, and we store it on the
group's behalf. That data is the roster, matches, goals, ratings, availability and fees. The people
it is about are entitled to expect you to look after it. So:

- Only add people who know they are being added, and record only what the group needs.
- If you add someone who has no account, tell them. They can't manage their own data, so they will
  come to you.
- When someone asks for a copy of their data, or to be removed, deal with it promptly. The app has
  the tools for both.
- Give roles only to people you trust. A manager or administrator can see and change other people's
  records.
- Be careful with phone numbers. They are for organising matches and nothing else.

their parent or guardian before adding them. They can't create their own account.

## 3. How we handle your group's data

For the data described above, we:

- process it only to run Sidekick for your group, and as the app's features instruct;
- keep it confidential, and secure it appropriately;
- use only the service providers named in the Privacy Policy, and tell you before adding others;
- help you answer requests from the people it is about, mainly through the tools in the app;
- tell you without undue delay if a breach affects your group's data;
- delete it when your group is deleted, apart from copies that stay in backups for a limited time.

## 4. Fees

If your group tracks fees, the ledger is your group's record. Sidekick never touches the money, and
it does not check what anyone enters. You are responsible for your group's money, and for any tax or
other duty it brings.

## 5. Chat and reports

Administrators review the messages reported in their group. Handle reports fairly and promptly, and
remove people who abuse others. Tell us about anything serious or illegal.

## 6. Matches

Your group's matches are yours to run: the pitch, the booking, the safety of the people playing, and
anything your venue or insurer requires. We do not organise or supervise them.

## 7. Deleting a group

A group is deleted when its last member leaves or deletes their account, after that person confirms.

**This can't be undone.** Everything in the group goes: matches, results, ratings, players, chat and
**all payment records**, including amounts still owed. We can't restore it. Download anything you
need first, and settle any money owed in the group.

While other members remain, the group can't be deleted, and its last administrator has to hand over
the role before going.

## 8. If a group breaks the rules

We may suspend or close a group that seriously or repeatedly breaks these terms or the Terms of
Service, or if the law requires it. We will tell its administrators why unless the law or safety
stops us.
